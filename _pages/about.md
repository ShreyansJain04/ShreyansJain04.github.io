## About

I graduated from IIT Gandhinagar in 2026 with a B.Tech in Electrical Engineering (Minor in AI) and now work as a BIIC Graduate at Barclays in Mumbai, applying ML and algorithms to equity execution.

Most of my research has been about **images that are hard to read**: ultrasound scans corrupted by therapy fields at the [MUSE Lab](https://labs.iitgn.ac.in/muselaboratory/), diffusion-model unlearning at **IISc Bangalore**, and LLM tutoring for GIS at **Purdue University**.
