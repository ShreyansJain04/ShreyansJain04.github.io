  1. Unlearning in Diffusion Models under Data Constraints: A Variational Inference Approach
    Subhodip Panda, Varun M S, Shreyans Jain, and 2 more authors
    Transactions on Machine Learning Research, 2026
    Earlier version at the NeurIPS 2024 Workshop on Safe Generative AI
    Abs arXiv HTML PDF

    We present Variational Diffusion Unlearning (VDU), a one-step framework that removes undesired classes or features from a pre-trained diffusion model using only the subset of training data to be forgotten. The loss combines a plasticity inducer, which lowers the log-likelihood of the undesired data, with a stability regularizer that preserves generation quality. We evaluate class unlearning on MNIST, CIFAR-10 and tinyImageNet with unconditional DDPMs, and feature unlearning (e.g., an artistic style) on pre-trained Stable Diffusion. An earlier version appeared at the NeurIPS 2024 Workshop on Safe Generative AI.

2025

  1. Improved Segmentation of Histotripsy Bubble Clouds Using Modern CNN Architectures and Combined Dice-Focal Loss
    Shreyans Jain, Soham Gaonkar, Aditya Borate, and 3 more authors
    2025
    Manuscript
    Abs Code

    Histotripsy is a non-invasive focused ultrasound therapy that mechanically ablates tissue through cavitation bubble clouds. A semantic segmentation framework was developed to segment histotripsy ablation regions from B-mode ultrasound, using a Combined Dice-Focal Loss to address the severe class imbalance of sparse bubble clouds. Six architectures were evaluated and compared against a prior ResNet-18 approach. EfficientNet-B0 U-Net with Combined Dice-Focal Loss achieved a mean IoU of 0.838 and global accuracy of 0.968 with 7.9M parameters; DeepLabV3 achieved a mean IoU of 0.812 as an upper-bound reference; both exceed the prior mean IoU of 0.765.

  2. VascFlexMap: Microvascular Ultrasound Imaging at Low Frame Rates Using Sparse Data and a Transformer-Decoder Network
    Ruchika Dhawan, Mihir Agarwal, Shreyans Jain, and 1 more author
    2025
    Manuscript. Ruchika Dhawan and Mihir Agarwal contributed equally
    Abs

    Super-resolution ultrasound reveals microvascular structures with exquisite resolution, but clinical translation is limited by the need for ultrafast frame rates, massive data volumes, and long reconstruction times. This work proposes a deep learning framework that reconstructs microvascular maps from low-frame-rate contrast-enhanced ultrasound sequences, bypassing explicit microbubble localization and tracking. A transformer-decoder network with learned linear projections models spatiotemporal dependencies across sparse sequences and reconstructs vessel probability maps refined by a post-processing stage. On in vivo rat brain bolus data from the PALA challenge, under up to 500-fold data reduction (341 frames at 2 FPS vs. 170400 frames at 1000 FPS), the pipeline recovered coherent microvascular architecture, with vessel widths broadened about three-fold relative to reference SR-US, and end-to-end reconstruction in 28-133 seconds on an NVIDIA H100 GPU.

  3. Suppressing Streak Artifacts Generated by the Interference of Imaging and Therapy Fields: Initial Findings Using a Hybrid U-Net and Diffusion Model
    Mihir Agarwal, Shreyans Jain, Ruchika Dhawan, and 2 more authors
    In IEEE South Asian Ultrasonics Symposium, Mar 2024
    Abs PDF Code

    We present a combined U-Net and diffusion model to remove streak artifacts in therapeutic ultrasound imaging. Using a denoising U-Net with skip connections and text-based prompts for enhanced inpainting, initial results show effective artifact removal, an improved Signal-to-Noise Ratio by 6.0 ± 3.4 dB, and an IOU score of 0.698. Rapid model convergence suggests potential for real-time clinical use. Future work will validate the model on diverse datasets and optimize performance.

  4. Reconstruction of Ultrasound Super-Resolution Images using a Hybrid Attention-based U-Net Architecture applied to sparse data
    Ruchika Dhawan, Mihir Agarwal, Shreyans Jain, and 2 more authors
    In International Society for Therapeutic Ultrasound Conference, Apr 2024
    Abs

    We developed a Hybrid Attention-Based U-Net architecture to enhance Ultrasound Localization Microscopy by accurately tracking microbubbles in noisy image sequences. Our approach reduces data requirements by 40 times and achieves a peak SNR enhancement of 14.73 dB in simulated data.
