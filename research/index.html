research

Medical ultrasound imaging, generative models and vision, with labs at IIT Gandhinagar, IISc Bangalore, Purdue and UChicago.

My research uses deep learning on images that are hard to read: ultrasound frames corrupted by therapy fields, sparse microbubble data, shadows, and generative models that need to forget. Most of my imaging work was done in the MUSE Lab with Prof. Himanshu Shekhar. Each entry links to a detailed page.

TMLR 2026

Variational Diffusion Unlearning

Prof. Prathosh A.P., IISc Bangalore May 2024 – Nov 2024
  • Framed unlearning in diffusion models (DDPM, Stable Diffusion) as variational inference: approximate the retrained posterior using only the samples to forget.
  • Two-term loss: a plasticity inducer pushes down the likelihood of unwanted data, and a stability regularizer prevents catastrophic forgetting.
  • Up to 71.6% unlearning (PUL) on CIFAR-10 with over 10× better u-FID than baselines. Published in TMLR 2026, after a NeurIPS 2024 workshop paper.

Improved Segmentation of Histotripsy Bubble Clouds Using Modern CNN Architectures and Combined Dice-Focal Loss

Prof. Himanshu Shekhar (IITGN) & Prof. Kenneth B. Bader (UChicago) 2024 – 2025
  • Built a semantic-segmentation framework for histotripsy bubble clouds in B-mode ultrasound, trained with a combined Dice + Focal loss for severe class imbalance.
  • Benchmarked six modern architectures; EfficientNet-B0 U-Net reaches mean IoU 0.838 and accuracy 0.968 (prior ResNet-18: 0.765 / 0.956) with only 7.9M parameters.
  • Fixed the prior model’s early-treatment blind spot; ~13.5 ms per image on an A100. Collaboration with Prof. Kenneth Bader (UChicago).

IEEE SAUS 2024 · ISTU 2024

Real-Time Streak Artifact Suppression in Therapy-Guidance Ultrasound

Prof. Himanshu Shekhar, MUSE Lab, IITGN Dec 2023 – Jul 2024
  • Designed a two-stage framework: multi-channel U-Net for streak segmentation, then a Fourier-attention U-Net for inpainting.
  • Real-time inference at >440 FPS with +13.7 dB reconstruction gain on synthetic streak data generated with Gaussian intensity modulation.
  • Presented at IEEE SAUS 2024 and ISTU 2024 (Taipei).

Manuscript · ISTU 2024

VascFlexMap: Microvascular Ultrasound Imaging at Low Frame Rates Using Sparse Data and a Transformer-Decoder Network

Prof. Himanshu Shekhar, MUSE Lab, IITGN 2024 - 2025
  • Transformer-decoder network that maps microvessels from contrast ultrasound acquired at only 2–50 FPS.
  • Up to 500× less data than conventional ultrasound localization microscopy, with 28–133 s reconstruction on one H100.
  • Earlier hybrid attention U-Net version presented at ISTU 2024 (40× data reduction, +14.73 dB peak SNR in simulation).

ICVGIP 2024

WavShadow: Wavelet Based Shadow Segmentation and Removal

Prof. Shanmuganathan Raman, IITGN Feb 2024 – Nov 2024
  • End-to-end shadow segmentation and removal: Haar-wavelet features with a SAM adapter for masks, FFC blocks with MAE priors for removal.
  • State-of-the-art 1.38 BER on ISTD; 36.7 PSNR on DESOBA (+0.7 over the 2023 state of the art).
  • First author (equal contribution), published at ICVGIP 2024.

Research internship · Purdue

GeoTutor: An AI Tutoring System for GIS Education with Hybrid RAG

Prof. Dharmendra Saraswat & Prof. Vaneet Aggarwal, Purdue University May 2025 – Jul 2025
  • Intelligent tutoring system for GIS education on a hybrid RAG pipeline: BM25 + dense FAISS retrieval + cross-encoder reranking, with citations on every answer.
  • Adaptive teaching engine with a learner-memory model: personalized plans, misconception detection, SuperMemo-2 spaced repetition.
  • FastAPI + Pydantic backend integrated with a React frontend; journal manuscript in preparation with Purdue faculty.
